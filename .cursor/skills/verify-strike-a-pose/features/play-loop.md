# Play loop

Framing shows Find your spot, Stand here, and Head, Arms, Legs. Countdown hides pause. The pose window shows the fit meter with the notch at 85, limb chips, the coach line, and pause. Pause offers Resume, Skip pose, and End game. Hold shows HOLD IT and Keep holding, and hides the chips. A miss snapshot shows SO CLOSE. A camera snap's JPEG is the mirrored frame with the caption burned in. Referee snaps stay a drawn stage. Handoff names the next player and says the camera is paused. Leaving the frame shows STEP BACK IN.
