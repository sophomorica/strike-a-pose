# Kids keep

A Kids game ends on Keep your faves. A heart keeps a snap. Keep all keeps every snap. Done with unkept snaps asks Delete N unkept snaps, with Back to keep and Delete and continue. Unfinished Kids games are deleted on the next launch. Other decks stay until someone deletes them.

Drive: `flutter test test/widget_test.dart --name "kids results"` and `flutter test test/engine_test.dart --name "kids end"`.
